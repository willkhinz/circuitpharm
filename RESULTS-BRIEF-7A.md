## 2. The 4-vs-3 Problem and What the Choice Discards

A 5-state Markov chain ($R, AR, A2R, A2O, A2D$) possesses 5 eigenvalues:
* $\lambda_5 = 0$: The stationary absorbing state (at zero agonist, all receptors eventually return to $R$). Its amplitude in the open state coordinate $A2O$ is identically $0$.
* 4 non-zero relaxation eigenvalues giving time constants $\tau_i = -1/\lambda_i$.

### Analytical Structure of the 4 Relaxation Modes:
At zero agonist, $[GABA] = 0$, so transitions $R \to AR$ and $AR \to A2R$ are zero. The generator matrix $Q(0)$ is upper block-triangular:
$$\begin{pmatrix} Q_{33} & Q_{32} & 0 \\ 0 & -k_{\text{off}} & k_{\text{off}} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$
where $Q_{33}$ governs the doubly-bound subsystem $\{A2R, A2O, A2D\}$, $-k_{\text{off}}$ governs unbinding $AR \to R$, and row $R$ is zero.

**Mathematical Theorem:**
Because unbinding from $AR$ cannot feed back into $A2R$ or $A2O$ at zero agonist, the unbinding mode $\lambda = -k_{\text{off}}$ has zero projection on the open channel coordinate:
$$(V^{-1})_{k_{\text{off}}, A2O} = 0 \implies a_{k_{\text{off}}} \equiv 0.0$$
The amplitude of this mode in the open state is **mathematically zero** (measured $< 10^{-16}$).

### Our Choice:
We rank the 4 components by absolute amplitude/area and select the **3 largest components**.

### What this choice discards:
* It discards the $\lambda = -k_{\text{off}}$ mode ($\tau = 1/k_{\text{off}}$).
* Because its amplitude in the observable $A2O$ is identically zero, **it discards ZERO observable current, ZERO open probability, and ZERO variance**.
* The remaining 3 components correspond exactly to the 3 eigenvalues of the $\{A2R, A2O, A2D\}$ coupled system:
  1. A fast mode ($\tau_1 \approx 0.03\text{--}0.9\text{ ms}$) with negative amplitude, capturing channel opening from $A2R$ immediately following pulse termination.
  2. An intermediate deactivation mode ($\tau_2 \approx 3\text{--}10\text{ ms}$).
  3. A slow resensitisation mode ($\tau_3 \approx 150\text{--}950\text{ ms}$).
